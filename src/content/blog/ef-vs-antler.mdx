## The core model

Both Entrepreneur First and Antler operate at the same stage: **pre-idea, pre-team**. They recruit exceptional individuals, help them find co-founders, and invest in the resulting companies. But the similarities mostly end there.

## Entrepreneur First

EF is the original talent investor. Founded in 2011, it has offices in London, Paris, Berlin, Bangalore, Singapore, and Toronto.

**The model:**
- 12-week cohort (Talent stage → Form → Launch)
- Intensive: you're expected to dedicate yourself completely
- Cohort size: ~80 people in London
- Investment: £80,000 for 10% equity (SEIS-eligible)
- Strong focus on deep tech, AI, and enterprise software

**What makes EF distinctive:**
EF is highly selective (3-5% acceptance rate) and specifically targets people who are exceptionally good at something — typically a domain expert, engineer, or commercial operator. The process of finding a co-founder is structured and rapid: you have weeks to form a viable pair before "Form" decisions.

**Who thrives at EF:**
- PhD-level domain experts
- Senior engineers or technical leaders
- People with deep industry expertise who lack a co-founder

## Antler

Antler launched in 2017 and has expanded to 30+ cities globally. The London programme is one of its strongest.

**The model:**
- 10-week cohort
- More self-directed: less structured than EF in the co-founder matching phase
- Cohort size: ~50 people in London
- Investment: £100,000 for 9% equity (SEIS-eligible)
- Sector-agnostic, though strong in fintech, consumer, and SaaS

**What makes Antler distinctive:**
Antler is more sector-agnostic and slightly less intense than EF. It also has a larger global network, which matters if you're targeting markets outside the UK. The Investment Committee decision happens at week 6-7.

**Who thrives at Antler:**
- Serial entrepreneurs
- Operators from high-growth companies
- People with strong commercial backgrounds

## Head-to-head comparison

| | EF | Antler |
|---|---|---|
| Founded | 2011 | 2017 |
| London cohort size | ~80 | ~50 |
| Equity | 10% | 9% |
| Investment | £80,000 | £100,000 |
| Duration | 12 weeks | 10 weeks |
| Sector focus | Deep tech, AI, enterprise | Agnostic |
| SEIS eligible | Yes | Yes |
| Application deadline | Rolling | Rolling |

## The honest answer

**Choose EF if:** You're a technical founder or domain expert who wants to be in the most demanding and prestigious programme in Europe, and you're building in deep tech, AI, or enterprise software.

**Choose Antler if:** You're a strong operator or commercial founder, you want slightly more flexibility in co-founder matching, or you're building in a sector EF doesn't specialise in.

**Apply to both.** The programmes overlap but don't conflict (different dates). Getting into both is a good problem to have.
