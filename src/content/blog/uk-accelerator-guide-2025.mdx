## What is a startup accelerator?

A startup accelerator is a fixed-term, cohort-based programme that invests in early-stage startups in exchange for equity. In return, startups get capital, mentorship, workspace, and — most importantly — access to a network of investors, alumni, and mentors.

The typical UK accelerator:
- Runs for 3-6 months
- Takes 5-10% equity
- Invests £25,000–£150,000
- Ends with a Demo Day for investor pitches

## Accelerators vs venture studios vs incubators

These terms are often confused:

**Accelerator** — Takes an existing (or forming) team, provides a structured programme, invests capital, ends with Demo Day. You keep control of your company.

**Venture studio** — Builds companies from scratch. Co-founds companies with founders, takes significantly more equity (15-30%), but provides much more hands-on support. EF and Antler technically operate a hybrid model.

**Incubator** — Usually less structured, longer duration, often equity-free or low equity. Barclays Eagle Labs is a good UK example.

**VC** — Not a programme. Just a fund that invests. Included in this directory because the process of finding which VCs are right for your startup is similar.

## Should you apply to an accelerator?

Accelerators are not right for everyone. Consider:

**Good reasons to apply:**
- You need your first institutional credibility to raise a seed round
- You need a co-founder (EF/Antler model)
- You're in a sector where the accelerator has strong portfolio/network (e.g. BGV for social impact)
- You're pre-product and need the structure and accountability

**Bad reasons to apply:**
- You're already growing and just need capital (apply directly to VCs)
- The equity ask is too high relative to what you'll get
- It's the only programme in front of you (which is why you should use this directory)

## The best UK accelerators by sector

### AI & Deep Tech
- **Entrepreneur First** — world-class for pre-idea technical founders
- **Google for Startups Accelerator UK** — equity-free, access to Google engineers
- **Wayra** — corporate accelerator for AI startups with B2B customers

### Social Impact & Climate
- **Bethnal Green Ventures** — UK's best impact accelerator, SEIS eligible
- **Founders Factory** — strong in Cleantech with corporate partnerships

### Fintech
- **Springboard Fintech** — backed by Mastercard and Barclays
- **Wayra UK** — Telefónica backing, enterprise fintech focus

### General / Stage-agnostic
- **Techstars London** — global network, Demo Day, strong US connections
- **Antler UK** — pre-idea to pre-seed, strong community

## What to look for when comparing accelerators

1. **Sector fit** — Does the accelerator have portfolio companies in your space? Mentors from your industry?
2. **Stage fit** — Are you at the right stage? Most accelerators are clear about this.
3. **Equity terms** — 5-10% is normal. Above 15% is high. Equity-free programmes exist for specific sectors.
4. **SEIS/EIS eligibility** — Matters a lot for angel fundraising. Check every listing on Accelerate.fyi.
5. **Cohort size** — Smaller cohorts (8-15) get more mentor attention. Larger cohorts (50-80) have more peer diversity.
6. **Alumni network** — This is the long-term value. Talk to alumni before applying.
